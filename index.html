LAB SD
Booking & Inventory System

A full-stack management platform built to facilitate the reactivation of a long-dormant Data Structure Laboratory. The system provides a smart booking solution for students and faculty to reserve individual PCs, workspace clusters, and lab equipment. It also adopts a cybercafe-style master-client architecture, enabling administrators to centrally monitor and manage client PCs remotely over a Wi-Fi network, bypassing traditional physical LAN constraints.

Laravel Livewire Master-Client Architecture Custom Client Scripts Wi-Fi Networking

PROBLEM: The university needed to reactivate a long-unused laboratory but lacked a structured system for students and faculty to reserve PCs, workspaces (clusters), and equipment. Additionally, the absence of functional LAN infrastructure prevented administrators from monitoring and controlling the lab computers centrally.

RESULT: Successfully facilitated the lab's reactivation by delivering a unified digital platform for real-time facility bookings and asset tracking. To solve the infrastructure limits, a custom Wi-Fi-based master-client system was engineered, enabling administrators to remotely control all client PCs without the need to overhaul the physical network cables.
